Results by schedule

Each split schedule gives five doses — two of measles, two of mumps, and one of rubella — at five visits, in the order its name lists them, with every second dose after the first. Thirty such schedules exist. The table compares coformulation with the best and worst split schedules and with any split schedule you choose. Effective reproductive numbers above 1 appear in red.

Fraction ever infected uses the endemic-equilibrium result s − 1/R₀, floored at zero, where s is effective population susceptibility. Break-even mortality equals mortality under the best split schedule minus mortality under coformulation. Break-even uptake is the visit 1 uptake the split schedules would need for the best of them to match a coformulated vaccine, holding every other assumption fixed.
